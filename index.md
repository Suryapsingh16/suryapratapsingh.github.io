## About Me

I am an R&D Engineer at QBit Labs Private Limited, working on digital signal processing and physical layer algorithm development for high-data-rate wireless communication systems.

My interests include wireless communications, MIMO-OFDM, OTFS, signal processing, and AI/ML for wireless systems. My work involves MATLAB-based simulations, channel estimation, time synchronization, channel tracking, and fixed-point modeling for modem development.

## Publications

### Hybrid Attention & SE-Net for CFO Estimation in MIMO-OFDM ABC Systems

- Developed a lightweight hybrid deep learning estimator for carrier frequency offset (CFO) estimation in ambient backscatter communication systems.
- Combined cross-attention and squeeze-and-excitation (SE-Net) blocks to process magnitude and phase inputs.
- Achieved an MSE of 10⁻⁶ at 25 dB SNR with approximately 1.11 million parameters across QAM modulations and 3GPP TDL channels.
- **Collaborators:** Dr. Udit Satija (IIT Patna) and Prof. Sudhan Majhi (IISc Bangalore).


### High-Mobility Emitter Identification Framework Using RF Fingerprints in OTFS

- Developed an OTFS-based emitter identification framework for high-mobility environments using convolutional neural networks (CNNs).
- Achieved identification accuracies of 98.79%, 97%, and 95.54% for 2, 3, and 4 emitters, respectively.
- **Collaborator:** Dr. Udit Satija (IIT Patna).

### Automatic Modulation Classification (AMC) in OTFS System

- Proposed an automatic modulation classification system for wireless physical layer applications.
- Classified 9 modulation schemes with 91.12% accuracy at 10 dB SNR.
- **Collaborator:** Dr. Udit Satija (IIT Patna).

### Operational Neural Network-Based LoRa Device RF Fingerprint Identification

- Investigated RF fingerprint identification for LoRa devices using operational neural networks.
- **Collaborator:** Dr. Udit Satija (IIT Patna).
